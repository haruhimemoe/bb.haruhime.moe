This page says what bb.haruhime.moe stores, why, and for how long.

## If you visit

We set no cookies unless you sign in, and run no analytics.

The editor saves your drafts, with the names you give them, in your own browser (its local storage), so they're there when you come back. They never reach our server unless you save one as a template. "Use" on a template leaves the filled-in text in the same place for the editor to open, and the editor removes it once it has.

Pictures, audio and videos in a preview load in your browser straight from wherever they're hosted, so that host sees your IP address when they load. We never fetch, pass through or store an image.

The collab maker runs entirely in your browser. The image you give it loads straight from its own host, like a preview's pictures, and bb never fetches, receives or stores it. The image's address, your regions and their links are saved in your own browser (its local storage), so they're there when you come back; "Clear" removes them. They never reach our server.

## osu! players and pools

The Players tool and the collab maker's "Link players" send the names or ids you paste to our server, which looks them up with the osu! API. We keep what osu! answers (each player's osu! ID, username and country, or that osu! has no such user) for 24 hours, an hour for names osu! doesn't know, so the same lookup doesn't ask osu! again. We don't keep who asked or what list they pasted.

Pool import asks pools.haruhime.moe for the pool you name, without any of your cookies, and asks osu! for its maps. We keep each map's artist, title, difficulty name, mapper and star ratings for a week.

To stop floods, "Use" on a template, player lookups and pool imports count per IP address (an IPv6 address by its /64), in counters that are deleted automatically within about two hours; so does our share of calls to osu!. The count of how often a template was used holds no IP address.

## If you sign in

Anyone with an osu! account can sign in, to save templates. Sign-in happens on [haruhime.moe](https://www.haruhime.moe), the account every haruhime tool shares: it stores your osu! user ID, username, avatar URL and country, the link to osu! (never osu! tokens) and your sessions, and its own privacy page covers them. bb only reads that account and session to know who you are, and writes nothing to it. bb itself stores the templates you save and your API key, if you make one.

When you're signed in, we also count, per account, how many template changes, reports and bb data deletions you make, in counters that are deleted automatically within about two hours. These counters use your osu! user ID, so deleting your bb data doesn't reset them.

haruhime.moe sets the session cookie on `.haruhime.moe`, so bb can read it, along with `haruhime-signed-in`, which pages can read. It only says "this browser may be signed in", so pages ask who you are only when it's there. bb sets neither cookie. How long a session lasts is up to haruhime.moe.

## Templates you save

A template keeps its name, description, kind, BBCode, fields, who owns it (osu! ID and username), how often it was used, and when it changed. Images and links inside a template are only their URLs: we never store the files. Public and unlisted templates can be seen by anyone with the link, and public ones are listed in the gallery with your osu! username. Private templates are seen only by you.

We keep every saved version of your templates (its content and who saved it and when) until you delete the template or your account. Only you can see this history unless you turn it public on the template's edit page, which lets anyone who can see the template see its history too. If you copied a template (a fork), your copy keeps the version of the original it started from, so we can offer pulling in later changes; if the original is deleted or made private, we just say its changes aren't available any more.

If you report a template, we keep your osu! ID, the reason and when, so you can report it only once. Only admins read reports. A public template three people report is hidden until an admin clears its reports; clearing keeps who reported it, so the same people can't hide it again.

## Deleting

"Delete my bb data" on your [bb settings page](/account) deletes your API key, every template you own and the reports on them, and the reports you made, at once. It also deletes those templates' history. Each template you reported then counts one report fewer. Deleting a template removes it, and its history, for good. Drafts in your browser stay there until you clear them. Your haruhime account itself (the osu! link and sessions) is deleted on [haruhime.moe/account](https://www.haruhime.moe/account); delete your bb data first if you want it gone too.

## What we never do

We don't sell or share any of this, and we don't host images.
