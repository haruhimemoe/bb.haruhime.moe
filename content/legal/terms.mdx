## The site

bb is a free, fan-made tool. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by ppy Pty Ltd, and we can change or stop any part of it. [Disclaimers](/legal/disclaimers) says more, including the warranty we don't give.

## Your account

Anyone with an osu! account can sign in, with osu!, on [haruhime.moe](https://www.haruhime.moe), the account every haruhime tool shares. Your account is your osu! account: we never see your osu! password, and your name and avatar come from osu!. [Privacy](/legal/privacy) says what we keep.

## Templates you save

You're responsible for what you put in a template: its name, description, BBCode, fields and the links and image URLs in it. Public and unlisted templates can be seen by anyone with the link, and public ones are listed in the gallery. Anyone who can see a template can use it and fork a copy of their own.

Don't use a template for slurs, hate, harassment, spam, malware links, or to pass yourself off as someone else, and only link images and media you're allowed to share. Names, descriptions and BBCode go through a content filter, but it only catches some of that.

## Moderation

Anyone signed in can report a template. A public template with three reports is hidden until an admin looks at it. Admins can clear reports, and remove templates or accounts that break these rules.

## Deleting

You can delete a template, or everything you keep on bb with "Delete my bb data" on your bb settings page, at any time. Your haruhime account itself is deleted on haruhime.moe/account. Deleting happens at once and can't be undone.

## Your rights

The [GDPR & CCPA page](/legal/your-privacy-rights) covers your rights over your data, and [Copyright & Takedown](/legal/copyright) covers reporting infringing content.
