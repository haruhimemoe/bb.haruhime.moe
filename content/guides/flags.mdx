osu! doesn't have a flag tag. A flag is a picture, so it goes in `[img]`, with the address of the flag image osu! itself uses.

## The quick way

In the editor, press Flag in the toolbar, search for a country by name or by its two-letter code, and click it. bb writes the `[img]` for you.

<Example source={"[img]https://osu.ppy.sh/assets/images/flags/1f1ef-1f1f5.svg[/img] [profile=2]peppy[/profile]"} />

## Current flags and old flags

osu! has two sets of flag images:

- The current flags are SVG files. Their address is made from the country code's two regional indicator letters, so Japan is `1f1ef-1f1f5.svg`.
- The old flags are PNG files named by country code, like `JP.png`.

The SVG flags have no fixed size, so a page shows them as wide as it can, which is why the one above is so big. The old PNG flags are small, about the height of a line of text.

bb uses the current flags by default. osu! shows every image through its own image proxy, and we can't check from here that the proxy passes SVG files. If a flag doesn't show on your page after you post, switch the picker to "Old (PNG)" and insert the flag again.

<Example source={"[img]https://assets.ppy.sh/old-flags/JP.png[/img] Japan, old style"} />

## Flags in a list of players

Put the flag right before the name, with a space between them, one player per line. The small PNG flags suit a list:

<Example source={"[img]https://assets.ppy.sh/old-flags/US.png[/img] [profile]first player[/profile]\n[img]https://assets.ppy.sh/old-flags/KR.png[/img] [profile]second player[/profile]"} />

Each current flag adds 65 characters to your post (an old one 49), so a long staff or player list is worth a look at the counter.
