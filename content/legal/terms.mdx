## The site

bb is a free, fan-made tool. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by ppy Pty Ltd. It comes as is: the preview can differ from what osu! shows, pages can be wrong or down, and we can change or stop any part of it.

## Your account

Anyone with an osu! account can sign in, with osu!. Your account is your osu! account: we never see your osu! password, and your name and avatar come from osu!. [Privacy](/legal/privacy) says what we keep.

## Templates you save

You're responsible for what you put in a template: its name, description, BBCode, fields and the links and image URLs in it. Public and unlisted templates can be seen by anyone with the link, and public ones are listed in the gallery. Anyone who can see a template can use it and fork a copy of their own.

Don't use a template for slurs, hate, harassment, spam, malware links, or to pass yourself off as someone else, and only link images and media you're allowed to share. Names, descriptions and BBCode go through a content filter, but it only catches some of that.

## Moderation

Anyone signed in can report a template. A public template with three reports is hidden until an admin looks at it. Admins can clear reports, and remove templates or accounts that break these rules.

## Deleting

You can delete a template, or your whole account with "Delete my account" on your account page, at any time. Deleting happens at once and can't be undone.

## Changes

We may change these terms. The date at the top says when they last changed.
