## Getting or deleting your data

"Delete my account" on your [account page](/account) deletes your account, every session, the link to osu!, every template you own and its history, and the reports you made or that were made on your templates. For anything else, email us.

Your name and avatar come from your osu! profile; change them there and bb picks up the new ones the next time you sign in.
