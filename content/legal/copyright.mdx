## What we can remove

We store the templates members save: their name, description and BBCode text. We don't host the images, audio or video a template's preview points at; those load straight from wherever the template links them, so a takedown for one of those goes to the host or site it's on. If a saved template's own text (its name, description or BBCode) infringes your rights, send a notice and we'll remove it.

## Beatmaps and osu! content

bb edits BBCode text; it doesn't host beatmaps. A problem with a beatmap itself belongs to osu! and its mapper, not to a template that merely links or describes it.
