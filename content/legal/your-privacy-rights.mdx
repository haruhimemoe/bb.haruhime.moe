## Getting or deleting your data

"Delete my bb data" on your [bb settings page](/account) deletes your API key, every template you own and its history, and the reports you made or that were made on your templates. Your haruhime account (the osu! link and sessions) is deleted on [haruhime.moe/account](https://www.haruhime.moe/account). For anything else, email us.

Your name and avatar come from your osu! profile; change them there and haruhime.moe picks up the new ones the next time you sign in.
