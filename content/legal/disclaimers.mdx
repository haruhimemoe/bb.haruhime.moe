## Estimates, not rulings

The editor's preview is our best match for how osu! renders BBCode, built by reading osu!'s own tag behavior; it can still differ from what osu! actually shows on a userpage, forum post or beatmap description. The character count against the 60,000-character limit is an estimate for the same reason. When in doubt, check the real page on osu!.

## Templates aren't reviewed before they're shown

A public or unlisted template is visible as soon as its owner saves it. Names, descriptions and BBCode go through a content filter, but it only catches some of what [Terms](/legal/terms) rules out; reports and admin review happen after the fact, not before.
