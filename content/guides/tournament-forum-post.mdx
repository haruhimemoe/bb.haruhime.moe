A tournament's forum post is where players look for everything: when it runs, who can join, the rules and the mappools. Most posts follow the same shape, and boxes keep a long one tidy.

## The shape

1. A banner or a heading, centred.
2. A short notice with the one thing people must not miss, like the registration deadline.
3. One box per topic: information, schedule, rules, prizes, staff, mappools and links.

<Example source={"[centre][heading]Example Cup 2026[/heading][/centre]\n[notice]Registrations close on October 12.[/notice]\n[box=Information]\n[list]\n[*]Mode: osu!\n[*]Format: 1v1, double elimination\n[*]Rank range: #10,000 to #50,000\n[/list]\n[/box]\n[box=Schedule]\n[list]\n[*]Registrations: October 1 to 12\n[*]Qualifiers: October 17 and 18\n[*]Grand finals: November 29\n[/list]\n[/box]"} />

## Rules

A numbered list keeps rules easy to point at in an argument. Any value after `list=` makes it numbered.

<Example source={"[box=Rules]\n[list=1]\n[*]Be on time for your match.\n[*]Warm-ups are one map, under five minutes.\n[*]Disconnects are replayed if they happen in the first 30 seconds.\n[/list]\n[/box]"} />

## Staff

Link every staff member to their profile, with their id so the link survives a name change. A flag in front of each name looks good in a staff list; the [flags guide](/docs/guides/flags) covers them.

<Example source={"[box=Staff]\n[b]Hosts[/b]\n[profile=2]peppy[/profile]\n\n[b]Referees[/b]\n[profile]someone[/profile]\n[/box]"} />

## Mappools

Give each round its own box, with one line per map: the slot, then a link to the beatmap. A mappool box per round lets people open only the round they're playing. Built the pool on [pools.haruhime.moe](https://pools.haruhime.moe)? The editor's Pool tool writes those lines for you, star ratings included, and [packs.haruhime.moe](https://packs.haruhime.moe) turns the pool into one download for your players.

## Before you post

- Check the counter. A big tournament post with many colored lines can come close to the 60,000 character limit.
- Every wavy underline in the editor is something that will look wrong on osu!.
- The built-in [tournament template](/t/bb-tournament-forum-post) has all of these sections ready to fill in.
